# Reports Module

## 1. Goal

Provide accurate printable and exportable attendance information.

## 2. Report Center

### Attendance

-   Daily Attendance
-   Monthly Attendance
-   Employee Attendance
-   Department Attendance
-   Late Employees
-   Absences
-   Undertime
-   Overtime
-   Incomplete Attendance

### Leave

-   Leave Summary
-   Leave Requests
-   Leave Balance

### Biometric

-   Enrollment Status
-   Device Logs
-   Synchronization Logs

## 3. Daily Attendance Report

Columns:

-   Employee ID
-   Employee Name
-   Department
-   Schedule
-   Time In
-   Time Out
-   Late
-   Undertime
-   Overtime
-   Status

Summary:

-   Present
-   Late
-   Absent
-   Leave
-   Rest Day
-   Holiday

## 4. Monthly Employee Report

Display each date and:

-   IN
-   OUT
-   Late
-   Undertime
-   Overtime
-   Status

Totals at the bottom.

## 5. Export

Support:

-   PDF
-   Excel
-   Print Preview
-   Direct Print

## 6. Report Filters

-   Date range
-   Employee
-   Department
-   Position
-   Status
-   Shift

## 7. Reporting Principle

Reports must use processed attendance records, while retaining the
ability to trace records back to raw biometric events and audit logs.
